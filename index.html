About me

Logic, creativity & empathy

I believe software should feel gentle to use and stay simple to change.

I'm Sasini, a full-stack developer working mostly with React, Node.js, Express and Spring Boot, with PostgreSQL and MongoDB underneath. Over two years at ShemailoSoft I helped build real client systems: a multi-vendor food ordering platform for a Finnish restaurant, a sports equipment store with more than 1,400 products, restaurant and retail POS systems, and booking tools.

Design is where I started falling in love with tech. I care about soft but professional palettes, clear user journeys and mobile-first layouts, because how a system feels is part of how well it works.
